<section id="questions" class="faq-section wrap section-space"><div><p class="eyebrow">04 / GOOD TO KNOW</p><h2>Clarity, before<br>commitment.</h2><a href="#rfq" class="text-link">Start with your requirements <span aria-hidden="true">↗</span></a></div><div class="faq-list">
<details><summary>Which service is right for me?<span aria-hidden="true">+</span></summary><p>Choose Connect if you want a supplier introduction and will manage the supplier yourself. Choose Source if you want Taotus to manage sourcing and supply the goods while you arrange shipping. Choose Deliver if you also want Taotus to coordinate delivery to your agreed destination. If you are unsure, choose “Help me choose” in your RFQ.</p></details>
<details><summary>Will I know the supplier’s identity?<span aria-hidden="true">+</span></summary><p>Connect is an introduction service: supplier details are handed over after the agreed fee is paid. With Source and Deliver, Taotus is your commercial counterpart and supplier identities are kept confidential. Supplier names and direct contacts are not included in buyer-facing shortlists or proposals. Any legally required disclosures and shipping documents are addressed in the agreed terms.</p></details>
<details><summary>Who handles purchasing and shipping?<span aria-hidden="true">+</span></summary><p>With Connect, you deal directly with the supplier after introduction. With Source and Deliver, Taotus buys from the supplier and resells to you under an agreed quotation. You arrange shipping with Source; Taotus coordinates door-to-door logistics with Deliver. Collection arrangements, delivery terms, import clearance, duties, and each party’s responsibilities are agreed before an order.</p></details>
<details><summary>How are fees and quotations agreed?<span aria-hidden="true">+</span></summary><p>Connect has an agreed introduction fee payable before supplier details are released. Source and Deliver are quoted for your specific requirements, with Taotus’s margin reflected in the commercial offer. Scope, payment terms, and any separately chargeable samples, inspections, freight, or duties are agreed before you commit.</p></details>
<details><summary>Can I request samples or specific checks?<span aria-hidden="true">+</span></summary><p>Yes. Include the samples, standards, certifications, or inspection requirements you need in your brief. Availability, the extent of checks, costs, and approval steps are confirmed as part of the scope. A request or quotation does not itself certify a supplier or a product.</p></details>
<details><summary>How do I contact Taotus from this preview?<span aria-hidden="true">+</span></summary><p>Use the RFQ builder above to organise your requirements and contact details, then copy or download the brief. This private preview does not yet have a connected enquiry inbox or online submission service. Preparing a brief does not send an enquiry or place an order.</p></details>
